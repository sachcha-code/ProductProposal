05 / Distribution & Integration

Seamless Integration.
Zero Added Friction.

IONIK's scope is end-to-end production — from design and manufacturing through quality control to sealed, branded, ready-to-issue packaging. Distribution to ticket holders is entirely SKG Events' responsibility, handled however they see fit. As Backstage, Gold, and Platinum ticket holders operate through dedicated entry channels at Port City Colombo, the handover process is operationally simple for the SKG ground team.

Option A
Dedicated Entry Lane Handover

Backstage, Gold, and Platinum ticket holders access the venue through exclusive, dedicated entry channels at Port City Colombo. SKG Events may leverage these existing lanes as the natural handover point — sealed units are issued directly upon arrival, enhancing the premium entry experience with no disruption to general flow.

Option B
Pre-Event Collection Window

SKG Events may organise a pre-event collection point where ticket holders receive their units before June 6th. This creates an early brand touchpoint and drives organic social content in the days leading up to the event — entirely at SKG's discretion to plan and coordinate.

IONIK's scope ends at production. Every unit is manufactured, quality-checked, and sealed in branded packaging — then delivered in full to SKG Events. Distribution from that point is managed entirely by SKG, in whichever way they see fit.
